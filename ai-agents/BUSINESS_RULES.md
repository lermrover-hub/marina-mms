# Business Rules — Ocean Rover Marina

Single source of truth for operational business rules applied by AI agents and the web app.
Agents must not override these rules. If a rule needs changing, update this file and the relevant `mms_agent_config` DB row.

Last updated: 2026-06-11

---

## 1. Boat Location Rules

**Rule BL-01: One active location at all times**
Every boat must have exactly one current active location at all times.
Valid locations: Wet Berth, Dry Storage, Repair Yard, Workshop Bay, Ramp Area, Waiting Area, Fuel Area, Temporary Holding Area.

**Rule BL-02: Movement requires log entry**
When a boat is moved, the system must:
1. Close the previous active location record (set end_date).
2. Create a new `mms_boat_movement_log` entry.
3. Update current location on the boat profile.
4. Require before/after photos for any major movement.

**Rule BL-03: AI agents may not change boat location**
No AI agent may change a boat's location. This is a physical operation that requires dockmaster sign-off.
The marina-agent may only raise an alert if a location record appears inconsistent.

## 2. Berth & Storage Slot Rules

**Rule BS-01: One active assignment per slot**
Each berth or storage slot may have many historical assignments but only one ACTIVE assignment at a time.
Supabase constraint enforces this at DB level.

**Rule BS-02: Slot status transitions**
Valid statuses: `available → reserved → occupied → maintenance | blocked`.
- `reserved`: booking confirmed, boat not yet arrived.
- `occupied`: boat physically present.
- `overdue_payment_hold`: payment is overdue; boat may not depart until cleared by Finance.

**Rule BS-03: Re-assignment requires cancellation first**
Before assigning a boat to a new slot, the current ACTIVE assignment must be set to CANCELLED or COMPLETED with an end_date.

## 3. Quotation Rules

**Rule QT-01: Quotation must link to customer**
Every quotation must have a `customer_id`. Boat ID is required when the service involves a specific vessel.

**Rule QT-02: Discount approval tiers**
| Discount % | Level | Required approval |
|---|---|---|
| 0 % | — | None |
| 1–2 % | — | None |
| 3–5 % | L3 | Marina Manager name + signature |
| 6–9 % | L2 | Marina Manager name + signature |
| 10–15 % | L1 | Marina Manager name + signature |
| > 15 % | BLOCKED | Cannot send — request must be restructured |

**Rule QT-03: AI-generated quotations require manager approval**
Quotations generated by an AI agent must go through the AI approval order flow:
`POST /api/ai/orders` → manager approves → `POST /api/ai/orders/:id/execute`.
Agents may not write to `mms_quotations` directly. Required approval role: `MANAGING_DIRECTOR`.

**Rule QT-04: Quotation status transitions**
`draft → pending_approval → sent → accepted | rejected | expired`
An accepted quotation may be converted to a work order, invoice, or contract.
A cancelled quotation cannot be reopened; a new one must be created.

**Rule QT-05: Overdue customer flag**
If a customer has invoices overdue > 30 days, the quotation agent must flag this in `notes` before creating the order. It does not automatically block the quotation.

## 4. Work Order Rules

**Rule WO-01: Work order cannot start without customer approval**
A work order must not be started until:
- Customer approval is recorded (quotation status = ACCEPTED, or direct approval on the work order).
- Deposit / payment condition is met (unless a manager explicitly overrides with a note).
- Scope of work is confirmed in writing.

**Rule WO-02: Work order tracks full cost**
Each work order must record: labour hours, material usage, contractor costs, and any other direct costs.
Job profitability = `revenue − labour_cost − material_cost − contractor_cost − other_cost`.
Margin % = `gross_profit / revenue × 100`.

**Rule WO-03: Photos required for major work**
Engine, structural, fiberglass, and painting jobs require photos in at least two categories:
`Before` and `After`. Defect photos are required when damage is discovered during work.

## 5. Invoice & Payment Rules

**Rule IP-01: Payment must link to invoice**
Every payment record must reference a valid invoice. Unlinked payments are not allowed.

**Rule IP-02: Invoice status driven by payments**
| Condition | Invoice status |
|---|---|
| No payment received | `issued` |
| Partial payment received | `partially_paid` |
| Full balance paid | `paid` |
| Due date passed, not fully paid | `overdue` |

**Rule IP-03: Outstanding balance calculation**
`outstanding_balance = total_amount − paid_amount`
The `outstanding_balance` column in `mms_invoices` must be kept in sync whenever a payment is recorded.

**Rule IP-04: Invoice cancellation**
Only Finance role may cancel an invoice. Cancellation requires a reason note.
A cancelled invoice cannot be reactivated; a credit note or new invoice must be issued.

**Rule IP-05: Receipt issued after confirmed payment**
A receipt is generated after payment status is set to `confirmed` by Finance.
AI agents may not mark a payment as confirmed.

**Rule IP-06: AI agents may not create invoices without approval**
Same approval order flow as quotations. Required role: `MANAGING_DIRECTOR` or `FINANCE`.

## 6. Ramp Booking Rules

**Rule RB-01: Tide safety check is mandatory**
Every launch and retrieval booking must include a tide safety calculation before confirmation.
The system must mark each hourly slot SAFE or UNSAFE and propose the earliest safe time.

**Rule RB-02: Tide formula**
See `TIDE_RULES.md` for the approved formula and operational values.

**Rule RB-03: Dockmaster gives final physical approval**
The system's SAFE classification is advisory only. Tide prediction may differ from actual sea level due to weather, wind direction, barometric pressure, and sea condition.
The dockmaster or operations supervisor must give verbal/written confirmation on the day of operation.

**Rule RB-04: Trailer and staff must be assigned before operation**
Ramp booking cannot move to IN_PROGRESS status until a trailer and at least one staff member are assigned.

**Rule RB-05: Payment or deposit required before launch**
Unless a manager overrides with a written note, payment or deposit must be confirmed before a launch or retrieval operation begins.

## 7. Approval & Escalation Rules

**Rule AP-01: Sensitive actions require audit log**
The following actions must create an entry in `mms_agent_audit_log`:
- Create / edit / delete quotation
- Approve quotation
- Cancel invoice
- Record payment
- Change boat location
- Close work order
- Modify pricing master
- Change user permission

**Rule AP-02: Agent errors must be escalated**
Any unhandled error in an AI agent must be escalated via `escalation-agent.js`, which creates a HIGH-priority notification. The CEO agent enforces this.

**Rule AP-03: AI messages are drafts until approved**
All outbound messages generated by the comms agent are saved as `PENDING_APPROVAL` in `mms_messages`.
No message is delivered to a customer until a staff member approves it in the UI.

---

## 8. Customer Portal Rules

**Rule CP-01: Customer sees only their own data**
Customer portal routes must filter by `customer_id` of the authenticated user.
No cross-customer data exposure is permitted.

**Rule CP-02: Customer may not approve their own quotation without staff confirmation**
Customer approval via portal sets quotation status to `PENDING_STAFF_REVIEW`, not directly to `ACCEPTED`.
Finance or Marina Manager must confirm before work begins.
